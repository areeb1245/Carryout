Profiles

A profile is a face, a card and an email address, kept in a SQLite file on this computer. A face that matches one is recognised as soon as it is in view.

    Their face as the camera sees it now is saved on this computer, and the card is saved to a new customer in the Stripe sandbox. The card is charged when they walk out, and the invoice goes to the email address.
